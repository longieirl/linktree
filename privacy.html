Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: 25 March 2026

Who we are

This is the personal links page of @longieirl, hosted at longieirl.github.io/linktree. It is a static website with no server-side processing and no user accounts.

What data we collect

We only collect data if you explicitly consent to analytics cookies. No data is collected by default.

If you accept analytics, Google Analytics 4 collects:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Referring website or source
  • Browser type and operating system (aggregated)
  • Country-level location (derived from a truncated IP address)

We do not collect names, email addresses, or any directly identifying information. We do not link analytics data to any other Google service.

Google Analytics configuration

To minimise data collection, Google Analytics is configured with the following settings:

  • IP anonymisation enabled — the last octet of your IP address is masked before storage
  • Advertising features disabled — Google Signals and ad personalisation signals are turned off
  • Restricted data processing enabled — limits how Google may use the data

Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy. You can opt out of Google Analytics across all sites using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser. This site uses the following cookies:

Name Purpose Duration Category
cookie_consent Stores your cookie preference so we don't ask again 1 year (localStorage) Essential
_ga Google Analytics — distinguishes unique visitors 2 years Analytics
_ga_* Google Analytics — maintains session state 2 years Analytics

Analytics cookies are only set after you give consent. You can change your preference at any time using the link.

Your rights

Under GDPR and similar laws you have the right to:

  • Withdraw consent at any time via Cookie Preferences in the footer
  • Request deletion of any data held about you
  • Object to processing of your personal data

To exercise these rights, or if you have any questions, contact jlongieirl+privacy@gmail.com.

Third-party links

This page contains links to external sites (GitHub, LinkedIn, etc.). Once you leave this site, their privacy policies apply. We are not responsible for their content or data practices.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to how we use cookies, we will update the date at the top of this page and re-prompt for consent where required.
